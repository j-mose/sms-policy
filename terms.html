SMS Terms and Conditions — Vision personal notification service

Last updated: 2026-09-03

Program description

Vision is personal automation software run by one individual on his own hardware. This SMS program sends that individual — and only that individual — automated notifications he configured the software to send him: reminders and alarms he created, and notices that a background job he started has stopped or failed.

There is exactly one recipient, who is also the operator and the account holder. There is no subscriber list, no sign-up, and no way for anyone else to receive these messages: the sending software has no field in which another telephone number can be entered.

How consent is given

The operator entered his own mobile number into a configuration file on his own computer. That act is the consent. No third party consents, is asked to consent, or can be messaged.

Message frequency

Low and irregular — typically a few messages per week, sent only when a reminder he created comes due or a job he started fails. The software enforces its own hard limit of 20 messages per day and a minimum gap of 60 seconds between messages.

Cost

Vision charges nothing. Message and data rates may apply from the recipient's own mobile carrier.

HELP

Reply HELP for help. Support is the operator himself, reachable at the email address on the associated Twilio account.

STOP

Reply STOP to stop receiving messages. Opt-outs are handled by Twilio's Advanced Opt-Out and take effect immediately. The operator can also stop all messages by removing his number from the software's configuration.

Carrier disclaimer

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery depends on the mobile carrier and is not guaranteed; this software treats SMS as best-effort and keeps every notification visible in its own interface as well, so that a dropped message loses no information.
