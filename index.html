Vision — automated notification service

Operated by John Mosley, sole proprietor.

Vision is a self-hosted task-automation and monitoring platform. Its notification feature delivers automated, transactional SMS generated by the software itself: reminders and alarms its user created, and notices that a background job has stopped or failed.

Application-to-person, not conversational

Messages are emitted by the application in response to events — a scheduler firing a reminder, a monitoring loop reporting a failed job. No person composes, types or sends them, and the program carries no marketing, promotional or conversational traffic.

One registered destination

Messages go only to the mobile number registered in the application's own configuration. The software exposes no field for any other destination, so no message can be addressed to a third party.

Frequency, cost and opting out

Typically a few messages per week, hard-capped by the application at 20 per day with a minimum of 60 seconds between sends. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to unsubscribe; START, YES or UNSTOP re-subscribe. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
