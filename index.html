Vision — personal notification service

Vision is personal automation software run by one individual, on his own hardware, for himself. Its SMS program sends that individual — and only that individual — automated notifications he configured the software to send him: reminders and alarms he created, and notices that a background job he started has stopped or failed.

There is exactly one recipient, who is also the operator and the account holder. There is no sign-up, no subscriber list, and no way for anyone else to receive these messages: the sending software has no field in which another telephone number can be entered.

Message frequency is low and irregular — typically a few messages per week, hard-capped by the software at 20 per day with a minimum 60 seconds between messages. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to unsubscribe. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
