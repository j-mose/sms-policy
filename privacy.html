Privacy Policy — Vision personal notification service

Last updated: 2026-09-03

Who this covers

Vision is personal automation software run by one individual, on his own hardware, for himself. There are no users other than the operator, no accounts, no sign-ups, and no customers. This policy exists because A2P 10DLC campaign registration requires a publicly reachable privacy policy (Twilio error 30933), and it describes honestly what the software does with the one piece of personal data it holds.

What data is collected

One mobile telephone number: the operator's own. He types it into a configuration file on his own computer. It is not collected from anyone, it is not obtained from a third party, and there is no form, page or interface through which anybody else's number can be entered — the software has no field for a second destination.

Alongside it, the software stores the text of each message it sends to that number, the delivery status returned by the carrier gateway, and the time of the attempt. That record exists so a message that failed to arrive can be seen and diagnosed.

No other personal data is collected. No names, no addresses, no contacts lists, no location, no advertising or analytics identifiers, and no tracking of any kind.

How it is used

Only to send the operator notifications he asked the software to send him: reminders and alarms he created, and notices that a background job he started has stopped or failed. Nothing is sent to anyone else, because there is nobody else and no way to name one.

Who it is shared with

Nobody, other than the messaging carrier required to deliver the message. Twilio Inc. acts as the delivery gateway and therefore receives the destination number and the message text; that is what sending an SMS means. Twilio's own privacy terms govern its handling of that data.

Beyond that: no sale, no rent, no sharing with third parties for any purpose, and specifically none for marketing. Mobile opt-in data and telephone numbers are never shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Where it is stored, and for how long

In a PostgreSQL database on the operator's own machine, reachable only from that machine. Records are kept until the operator deletes them; he can delete any or all of them at any time, since it is his database.

Message and data rates

Standard carrier message and data rates may apply to messages received.

Opting out

The operator can stop all messages by replying STOP to any message, or by removing his number from the software's configuration. There is no other subscriber to opt out.

Contact

The operator of this software is the only party to contact, at the email address on the associated Twilio account.
