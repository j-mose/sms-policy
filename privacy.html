Privacy Policy — Vision notification service

Last updated: 2026-09-04

What this software is

Vision is a self-hosted task-automation and monitoring platform. Its notification feature sends automated, transactional SMS — reminders and alarms its user created, and notices that a background job has stopped or failed — to the mobile number registered in the application's own configuration. The messages are generated by the software in response to events; no person composes or types them.

This policy describes what that software does with the one piece of personal data it holds.

What data is collected

One mobile telephone number: the number its user registered in the application's configuration. It is not collected through a form, not purchased, and not obtained from any third party. The application exposes no field for a second destination, so no other person's number can enter the system.

Alongside it, the application stores the text of each message it sends, the delivery status returned by the messaging carrier, and the time of the attempt, so that a notification which failed to arrive can be seen and diagnosed.

No other personal data is collected: no names, no addresses, no contact lists, no location, no advertising or analytics identifiers, and no tracking of any kind.

How it is used

Only to deliver the notifications the user configured the software to send to that number. Nothing is sent to any other destination, because the application has no way to name one.

Who it is shared with

Nobody, other than the messaging carrier required to deliver the message. Twilio Inc. acts as the delivery gateway and therefore receives the destination number and the message text; that is what sending an SMS means, and Twilio's own terms govern its handling of that data.

Beyond that: no sale, no rent, and no sharing with third parties for any purpose. Mobile opt-in data and telephone numbers are never shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Where it is stored, and for how long

In a PostgreSQL database on the machine that runs the software, reachable only from that machine. Records are kept until deleted by the operator of that machine, who can remove any or all of them at any time.

Message and data rates

Standard carrier message and data rates may apply to messages received.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message; opt-outs are handled by Twilio Advanced Opt-Out and take effect immediately. Removing the number from the application's configuration also stops all messages.

Contact

The operator of this software can be reached at the email address on the associated Twilio account.
